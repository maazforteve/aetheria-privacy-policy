Privacy Policy: Aetheria: Beast Wardens

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Aetheria: Beast Wardens ("the game") handles information. In short: the game does not collect, share or sell your personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and does not contain advertising or analytics software.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (beasts, stages cleared, settings and similar game data) is saved only in the game's private storage on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Permissions

The game requests only the vibration permission, used for haptic feedback during play. It does not request network, location, camera, microphone, storage or contacts access.

Third parties

The game does not share information with third parties and does not include third-party advertising, tracking or analytics services. Google Play itself may process data under Google's own privacy policy when you install or update the game.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game later adds online features that handle data, this policy will be updated before they are released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact details shown on the game's Google Play store listing.
